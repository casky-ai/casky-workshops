How this works

Every exercise here — the scenario, the evidence, the wiki, the scripts — is free and open-source in this repo, permanently. What's for sale is live delivery: a seat in a real session, taught in person or virtual, working through the material with an instructor and a cohort. A short free "Lightning Lesson" runs periodically as a preview of the format before any paid session — same content, shorter and live.
